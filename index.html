About Me

Storyteller by Instinct,
Designer by Craft

I'm a creative video editor and graphic designer driven by a passion for visual storytelling, cinematic motion, and modern design aesthetics. I explore different styles of editing, motion graphics, and digital visuals to create work that feels engaging, expressive, and impactful.

I enjoy transforming simple ideas into visuals that connect with people whether through pacing, sound, composition, or design. Constantly experimenting with new creative workflows, tools, and techniques, I see every project as an opportunity to learn and push creative boundaries.

Currently pursuing my Masters in Journalism & Mass Communication at Noida International University, I blend journalistic depth with design sensibility to create work that is both meaningful and memorable.
